Record offline speech bundle v1

Recognition: SenseVoiceSmall by Alibaba Group / FunAudioLLM / ModelScope.
Source: https://huggingface.co/FunAudioLLM/SenseVoiceSmall
License: FunASR Model Open Source License Agreement v1.1 (included).
Packaged model: sherpa-onnx-sense-voice-zh-en-ja-ko-yue-int8-2024-07-17.
asr.onnx is the upstream int8 ONNX export, renamed from model.int8.onnx;
tokens.txt is unchanged. No model fine-tuning was performed by Record.

Segmentation: pyannote/segmentation-3.0 by pyannote / CNRS.
Authors: Alexis Plaquet, Herve Bredin; trained by Severin Baroudi.
Source: https://huggingface.co/pyannote/segmentation-3.0
License: MIT (upstream license included).
segmentation.onnx is the sherpa-onnx ONNX export, renamed from model.onnx.
Citation: Plaquet and Bredin, Powerset multi-class cross entropy loss for
neural speaker diarization, INTERSPEECH 2023.

Voice embeddings: 3D-Speaker ERes2Net by Alibaba Group / ModelScope.
Source: https://github.com/modelscope/3D-Speaker
Original model: iic/speech_eres2net_base_sv_zh-cn_3dspeaker_16k.
License: Apache-2.0 (included).
embedding.onnx is the sherpa-onnx export renamed from
3dspeaker_speech_eres2net_base_sv_zh-cn_3dspeaker_16k.onnx.
Embeddings are only held in memory during one recording's transcription.

Inference: sherpa-onnx v1.13.8, Apache-2.0.
Source: https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx
Copyright contributors, including Xiaomi Corporation.
Bundled ONNX Runtime by Microsoft Corporation, MIT license (included).
Android wrapper uses Kotlin (JetBrains), Apache-2.0.

Model conversion and public downloads are provided by the k2-fsa
sherpa-onnx project. Model filenames were changed for packaging only.
Models can misrecognize speech and speaker counts; speaker IDs are estimates
and do not identify real people. Record does not upload audio to the AI API.
